Programme Implementation

Build Career Readiness. Build Verified Points.

The grant is designed to encourage schools to move from occasional career counselling toward a structured, continuous and measurable career-readiness ecosystem for students of Classes IX–XII.

01 · Awareness
Career Awareness · 10 Points

Helping students explore pathways and possibilities.

Structured career awareness sessions introduce students to the breadth of pathways available after school — from traditional academic streams to emerging fields, vocational routes and new-age careers.

02 · Assessment
Assessment & Guidance · 25 Points

Objective insight into strengths, interests and aptitudes.

Career and aptitude assessments give students a clearer picture of their strengths, and help counsellors and teachers guide stream selection and subject choices with more confidence.

03 · Parents
Parent Engagement · 15 Points

Aligning families with the student's future.

Parent sessions bring families into the conversation — helping them understand new-age career paths, the changing higher-education landscape, and how to support informed decisions at home.

04 · Real World
Exposure · 15–25 Points

From the classroom to the real world.

Campus visits, industry visits and career fairs give students first-hand exposure — turning abstract career conversations into lived experiences they can draw on when making decisions.

Before Your School Enquires

Know this about the grant.

Clear, factual conditions so your school can make an informed decision.

1
Registration does not automatically guarantee a grant.

Grant is confirmed only after applicable participation, documentation, verification and programme-completion requirements are met.

2
Points are awarded only for approved and verified activities.

Activities must be conducted within the approved programme period and meet verification requirements.

3
Student participation and documentation matter.

Evidence — attendance, photographs, activity summary, assessment records, visit details — is required for points to be awarded.

4
Final grant level is confirmed after verification.

MMC may validate activity quality, participation and documentation before awarding points and confirming the applicable grant level.

5
The grant is intended for institutional and student-development purposes.

It supports the school's career-readiness implementation and student-development activities.

6
Not a personal payment or commission.

The grant is not a personal payment or commission to any individual associated with the school.
